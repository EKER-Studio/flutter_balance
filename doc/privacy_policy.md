# Privacy Policy for Balance: BMI & Weight Tracker

**Effective date:** August 20, 2026  
**Last updated:** August 20, 2026

EKER Studio ("we", "our", or "us") built the **Balance: BMI & Weight Tracker** app as a free/ad-free utility app. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our application.

### 1. Information Collection and Storage
- **Personal & Health Data:** All health-related metrics (such as weight entries, height, BMI calculations, target weight, and calendar logs) entered into the app are stored exclusively **locally on your device**. We do not operate external databases or servers that collect, store, or transmit your health measurements.
- **Biometric Data:** If you enable biometric lock (Fingerprint or Face Unlock), authentication is processed entirely by your device's operating system. The app does not access, collect, or store your biometric data.

### 2. Google Health Connect Integration
- If you grant explicit permission, Balance: BMI & Weight Tracker integrates with Google Health Connect to read and sync body weight and height measurements.
- Health Connect data is accessed only with your direct consent and is used strictly to display metrics within the app.
- We do not sell, transfer, or share your Health Connect data with any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

### 3. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics
- The application may collect anonymous technical crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose defects and improve app stability. These logs do not contain personal health entries or identifiable sensitive data.

### 4. Third-Party Access and Data Sharing
- We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.
- All backups (such as CSV export) are generated locally and remain under your full control.

### 5. Medical Disclaimer
- Balance: BMI & Weight Tracker is intended for personal health tracking and informational purposes only. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any health condition or disease.

### 6. Children's Privacy
- Our app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
